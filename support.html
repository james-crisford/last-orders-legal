Last Orders - Support

Last Orders is a small app for one group of friends to log drinks, keep a shared board, and get a funny morning-after report.

Get in touch

Something broken, a question, or feedback: 3dprintquotes@gmail.com

Common questions

I lost my phone - can I get my history back?

No. There's no account to sign back into on purpose - see the Privacy Policy for why. Your history lives on the phone that logged it, plus whatever you'd already saved as a webpage from Settings → Your stuff.

The camera couldn't name my drink.

You can always tap it in yourself - the photo still logs, it just needs a name. This usually happens when the shot is unclear, or (rarely) when the app's daily AI quota has run out for the day.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Your stuff → Delete everything. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what that does and does not remove.

How do I leave a group?

Settings → Leave the pub.
