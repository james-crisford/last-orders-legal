Four places. One board. Same night.

Last Orders is one shared board for one group of friends. Snap your drink and the app names it. It lands on everyone's board at once, so the ones who moved away are still at the table. No feed, no strangers. Just your lot, keeping count together.

There's more on the back

Turn the mat over.

Six more things your pub keeps for you.

1/6

Tonight

One board for the night.

Everyone who's out, wherever they're out, on one live board. Snap your drink and the app names it. The night sits on your Lock Screen too, as a Live Activity.

2/6

The World

Every country you've shared a drink in.

Holidays, work trips, the one who moved abroad. It all lands on your group's globe. Drag to spin it.

3/6

The Passport

Stamped, one country at a time.

Every new country gets a stamp, with the date and what was in the glass.

4/6

The Cap collection

A wall of caps you earn.

Name the brand and its cap goes up on your wall. A cap is a brand, not a category.

5/6

Memories

The nights worth keeping.

A scrapbook of your best nights: who was there, and what was new.

6/6

The landlord

Short, dry, warm.

A fresh line from behind the bar every day. And the morning after, the Damage Report: a write-up of the night, and who might need a check-in.
