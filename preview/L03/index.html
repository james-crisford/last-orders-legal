On the board

Six things your pub gets. All real, all in the app. Screens below are from the app with made-up mates.

  1. The live boardserved live

    Tonight's board, shared. Snap your drink and the app names it. Everyone in your pub sees the same night, wherever they're sat.

    It's the one pinned up top, with Ollie circled.

  2. Memoriesthe good ones, kept

    A scrapbook of the nights worth keeping: who was there, and what was new to you.

  3. The Worldserved worldwide

    Every country your group has shared a drink in, on a globe you can spin. Each one gets a stamp in the Passport.

  4. My Pubserved by the day

    A beer-mat dial of your days. The more mates were there, the brighter the day glows. Press one and that night develops like a polaroid. Tap the middle for a fact.

    That's the real thing playing, frame by frame.

  5. The cap wallone per brand

    Name the drink and its bottle cap goes on your wall. A little record of what your lot have tried.

  6. The Damage Reportserved the morning after

    The landlord writes up the night in short, dry lines. Who turned up, and who might need a check-in.

Also on the board: a Lock Screen card for the night, and a landlord who says it in one line.
