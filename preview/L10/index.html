Why your lot will want it

Same night. Different cities.

It's for people who go out together, and the ones who moved away. Nobody's competing. You're just keeping count of the good nights, together.

1

Half of you moved away.

Leeds, London, Lisbon, wherever. Everyone logs what they're having on one live board, so the whole group is looking at the same night.

2

Snap it. It's named.

Point the camera at your drink and the app works out what it is. No typing it out at the bar.

3

A cap for every new brand.

Name the drink and its cap lands on your wall. A cap is a brand, not a number, so the wall remembers what you've tried, not how much.

4

A stamp for every country.

Share a drink abroad and the group's passport gets a stamp. The world map fills in with everywhere your lot have raised one.

5

Someone checks in after.

The morning after, the Damage Report shows who might need a check-in. Looking out for each other, same as always.

6

The good nights get kept.

Memories is a scrapbook of your best nights: who was there, and what was new. The landlord chips in with a dry line or two.
