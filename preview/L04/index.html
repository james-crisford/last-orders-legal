Tonight

Same night, different postcodes.

Snap your drink and the app names it. It lands on tonight's board, live, so the ones at the bar and the one who moved to Bristol are all looking at the same night.

While it's on, the night sits on your Lock Screen too.

My Pub + Memories

Press a day. Watch the night develop.

My Pub is a beer-mat dial of your days. The more mates were out, the brighter a day glows. Press one and that night develops like a polaroid in the middle. Tap the centre and it flips for a fact.

Memories

A scrapbook of the nights worth keeping: who was there, and what was new to you.

The World + the Passport

Every country you've shared a drink in.

Spin the globe and see everywhere the group has been. Each new country gets a stamp in your passport, from the local to the holiday to the wedding abroad.

The Cap collection

Name the drink. Keep the cap.

Name the brand and its bottle cap goes up on your wall. A little souvenir of what you've tried and where you tried it. Not a scoreboard.

The morning after

The morning after, someone checks in.

The Damage Report writes the night up in the landlord's dry hand, and shows who might want a check-in. Looking out for each other, not keeping score.
