Behind the bar

Real screens from the appDemo mates, real UI

01

Tonight

The live board for the night. Snap your drink with the camera and the app names it. Your mates see it straight away, wherever they are.

Ollie's on a Guinness. Mads went for the Sauvignon.

My Pub

02

A beer mat of your days.

My Pub turns your month into a dial. Each tick is a day, and the more mates were there, the brighter it glows.

Tap the middle.

The centre flips over for a fact about your pub. This one: International Waters unlocked.

Press a day.

That night develops in the middle like a polaroid. Sun 4 Oct, with Dave and four more.

Real app frames
03

Memories

A scrapbook of the nights worth keeping. Who was there, and what was new.

04

The World

Every country your group has shared a drink in, lit up on a globe you can spin. Each one gets a stamp in the Passport.

05

Lock Screen

While the night's on, a Live Activity card sits on your Lock Screen. Glance at it, put the phone away, get back to the conversation.
