Last Orders - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Last Orders is a small app built for one group of friends to log drinks on a night out, see a shared board, and get a funny morning-after report. This page explains what it collects and why, in plain English.

Who runs this

Last Orders is built and operated by James Crisford. Contact: 3dprintquotes@gmail.com.

There is no account, no email, no password

You never give Last Orders your name, email address, or a password. When you open the app you're signed in anonymously and given a random device identity. The nickname and photo you set are just how the group sees you - they're not tied to any real-world identity Last Orders asks for.

What the app collects

  • Your nickname and avatar (an emoji, or a photo you choose to add).
  • Drinks you log: type, time, and - only if you choose to attach one - a photo or short video.
  • Group membership: which group(s) you're in, and basic activity inside them (reactions, votes on a Fastest Pint run, badges earned).
  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications, so the app can tell you when something happens in your group.
  • Basic usage events (which screens you open, whether a feature worked) - used only to find and fix bugs, never sold or shared with advertisers. There are no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in this app.

Photos, video, and the drink-scanning feature

If you take a photo of a drink, that photo is sent to a third-party AI service (currently one of Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, or OpenAI, depending on which is running that day) to identify what's in it, so the app can log it automatically. The photo is also stored so it can be shown on your group's board (that's the point of taking it). Nobody outside your group sees it.

If you film a Fastest Pint run, that video (and the microphone audio that comes with it) is stored the same way as a photo, for the same reason: so your group can watch it back. It is not sent to any AI service.

Where this is stored

Everything above is stored using Supabase, a cloud database and file storage provider. Last Orders does not sell your data, does not share it with advertisers, and does not use it for anything beyond running the app for your group.

Who can see what

Everything you log is visible to the other members of your own group only. There is no public feed, no discovery of other groups, and no way for someone outside your group to see your drinks, photos, or activity.

Deleting your data

Settings → Your stuff → Delete everything removes your name, photo, and device link permanently, in every group you're a member of, not just the one you happen to be standing in. Drinks you logged stay on each group's board as an anonymous entry (a "former regular, no name attached") so the shared history of a night out isn't erased for everyone else who was there - but nothing that identifies you stays attached to them. This cannot be undone.

Children

Last Orders is about logging alcoholic drinks and is not intended for anyone under the legal drinking age in their country, or under 17.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change with it.
